# Selected Du'as for Hajj & Umrah

**Disclaimer: Verify Arabic text and translations with qualified scholars.**

## Talbiyah (During Ihram)

### Arabic
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لاَ شَرِيكَ لَكَ

### Translation
"Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Indeed all praise, favor and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner."

**Source**: Bukhari, Muslim

## During Tawaf

### Between Yemeni Corner and Hajar al-Aswad
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

"Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the next world, and save us from the punishment of the Fire." (Qur'an 2:201)

**Source**: Abu Dawud, Ahmad

### General Tawaf Du'a
- No specific du'as required for each round
- Recite Qur'an, dhikr, or personal du'as
- Keep voice low to avoid disturbing others

## At Safa and Marwah

### Upon reaching Safa/Marwah
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ وَهَزَمَ الأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ

"Allah is greatest, Allah is greatest, Allah is greatest. None has the right to be worshiped but Allah alone, Who has no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is Able to do all things. None has the right to be worshiped but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His slave, and defeated the confederates alone."

**Source**: Muslim

## Standing at Arafah

### Recommended Du'a
اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ كَالَّذِي نَقُولُ وَخَيْراً مِمَّا نَقُولُ، اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ صَلاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي وَإِلَيْكَ مَآبِي وَلَكَ رَبِّ تُرَاثِي

"O Allah, to You is all praise as we say and better than what we say. O Allah, to You is my prayer, my sacrifice, my life and my death, and to You is my return, and to You, my Lord, is my inheritance."

### Best Du'a at Arafah
خَيْرُ الدُّعَاءِ دُعَاءُ يَوْمِ عَرَفَةَ وَخَيْرُ مَا قُلْتُ أَنَا وَالنَّبِيُّونَ مِنْ قَبْلِي لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

"The best du'a is the du'a of Arafah, and the best thing that I and the prophets before me have said is: 'None has the right to be worshiped but Allah alone, Who has no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is Able to do all things.'"

**Source**: Tirmidhi

## During Ramy (Stoning)

### At each Jamrah
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

"Allah is greatest" (with each stone)

### After Small and Middle Jamrah
- Stand and make du'a facing qiblah
- Raise hands and make personal supplications
- No specific du'a required

## General Supplication
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي وَخَطَئِي وَجَهْلِي

"My Lord, forgive my sins, my mistakes, and my ignorance."

**Note**: These are selected du'as. Personal supplications in any language are also encouraged throughout the pilgrimage.

**Sources**: Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Sunan Abu Dawud. Verify exact wording with local scholars.

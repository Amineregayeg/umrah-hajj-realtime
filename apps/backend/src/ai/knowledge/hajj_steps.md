# Hajj Steps Guide

**Disclaimer: This is educational guidance only. Follow local Hajj authority instructions for timings and safety.**

## Types of Hajj
1. **Tamattu'** - Most common: Umrah first, exit ihram, then Hajj
2. **Qirān** - Combined intention for Umrah and Hajj, stay in ihram
3. **Ifrād** - Hajj only intention

## Hajj Tamattu' Timeline (Typical Schedule)

### 8th Dhul-Hijjah (Tarwiyah Day)
- **Morning**: Enter ihram for Hajj from Mecca
- **Forenoon**: Travel to Mina
- **Stay**: Night in Mina (recommended)
- **Activities**: Pray 5 prayers shortened, prepare for Arafah

### 9th Dhul-Hijjah (Arafah Day) - Most Important
- **Dawn**: Fajr in Mina, then travel to Arafah
- **Zuhr-Asr**: Combined and shortened at Arafah
- **Afternoon**: Stand at Arafah (wuquf) - THE PILLAR OF HAJJ
- **Sunset**: Leave for Muzdalifah (no Maghrib in Arafah)
- **Night**: Pray Maghrib-Isha combined in Muzdalifah, sleep under stars

### 10th Dhul-Hijjah (Eid Day)
- **Dawn**: Fajr in Muzdalifah, collect pebbles
- **After sunrise**: Travel to Mina
- **Morning sequence**: Ramy Jamrah al-Aqabah (7 stones) → Sacrifice → Halq/Taqsir → Tawaf al-Ifadah → Sa'i (if not done before)

### 11th-12th Dhul-Hijjah (Tashriq Days)
- **Stay**: In Mina (can leave after Zuhr on 12th if desired)
- **Daily**: Stone all three Jamarat (21 stones total each day)
- **Order**: Small, Middle, Large Jamrah

### Before Departure
- **Tawaf al-Wada'** (Farewell Tawaf) - required for non-Meccans

## Key Differences by Type

### Qirān vs Tamattu'
- **Qirān**: No exiting ihram between Umrah and Hajj
- **Qirān**: Must do sa'i twice OR once after Tawaf al-Ifadah
- **Both**: Require sacrifice (hady)

### Ifrād
- No Umrah component
- No sacrifice required
- One sa'i after Tawaf al-Ifadah

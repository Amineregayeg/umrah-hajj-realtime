# Madhhab Differences in Hajj & Umrah

**Disclaimer: Verify locally with qualified scholars from your madhhab.**

## Intention (Niyyah) Formulations

### Hanafi
- Emphasis on heart intention, verbal declaration optional
- Specific wording less critical than sincere intention

### Maliki  
- Heart intention sufficient, but verbal statement recommended
- Focus on clarity of purpose

### Shafi'i & Hanbali
- Both heart intention and verbal declaration recommended
- More specific about pronunciation of intention

## Tawaf Differences

### Starting Point
- **Majority**: From Hajar al-Aswad line
- **Some positions**: Slight variations in exact positioning acceptable

### Ramal (Brisk walking)
- **Hanafi**: First three rounds of any tawaf
- **Others**: Generally first three rounds of Tawaf al-Qudum only

### Istilam (Touching/Kissing stones)
- **All madhahib**: Kiss if possible, otherwise point and say "Allahu Akbar"
- **Variations**: Slight differences in preferred hand gestures

## Sa'i Positioning

### Hanafi
- Can perform sa'i before Tawaf al-Ifadah in Hajj
- More flexibility in sequencing

### Shafi'i/Hanbali
- Prefer sa'i after tawaf in proper sequence
- Stricter about order of rites

### Maliki
- Moderate position between the above

## Ramy (Stoning) Timings

### Hanafi
- More lenient time windows
- Allow stoning throughout day and night

### Shafi'i/Hanbali  
- Prefer specific time windows
- More restrictive about optimal times

### All Schools
- Agree on validity throughout Tashriq days
- Accommodate genuine need and crowding

## Hair Cutting (Halq/Taqsir)

### Length Requirements
- **Hanafi**: At least finger-joint length for taqsir
- **Others**: Shorter amounts acceptable
- **All**: Complete shaving (halq) preferred for men

## Sacrifice (Hady) in Tamattu'/Qirān

### Hanafi
- Specific about types of animals acceptable
- Detailed age requirements

### Others
- Generally similar requirements
- Slight variations in alternatives for poor

## General Principle
All madhahib agree on core obligations (arkān). Differences mainly in:
- Optimal timings
- Sequence flexibility  
- Recommended practices (mustahab)
- Penalty (dam) calculations

**When in doubt**: Follow local imam's guidance or the dominant madhhab in your area.

**Sources**: Classical fiqh texts, contemporary scholars' comparisons. Always consult qualified local authority for specific situations.

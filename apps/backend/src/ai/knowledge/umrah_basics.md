# Umrah Basics

**Disclaimer: This is educational guidance only. Consult qualified scholars for binding religious rulings.**

## Pillars (Arkān) of Umrah
1. **Ihram** - State of consecration with intention (niyyah)
2. **Tawaf** - Circumambulation of the Ka'bah (7 rounds)
3. **Sa'i** - Walking between Safa and Marwah (7 rounds)
4. **Halq/Taqsir** - Shaving or trimming hair

## Wajibat (Obligations)
- Entering ihram from designated meeqat
- Performing tawaf starting from Hajar al-Aswad
- Covering the required distance in sa'i
- Maintaining state of ihram throughout

## Typical Order of Rites
1. **Preparation**: Ghusl, wear ihram garments, make niyyah at meeqat
2. **Arrival**: Enter Masjid al-Haram with right foot, recite du'a
3. **Tawaf**: Start at Hajar al-Aswad, circle Ka'bah 7 times counterclockwise
4. **Prayer**: Pray 2 rak'ahs at Maqam Ibrahim (or anywhere if crowded)
5. **Sa'i**: Walk between Safa and Marwah 7 times (starting at Safa)
6. **Completion**: Halq (shaving) or taqsir (trimming) - men typically shave

## Ihram Do's and Don'ts

### Prohibited in Ihram:
- Cutting hair or nails
- Using perfume or scented products
- Covering head (men) / covering face (women - scholarly differences)
- Sexual relations or talk
- Hunting land animals
- Wearing sewn garments (men)

### Recommended:
- Frequent dhikr and du'a
- Reciting Talbiyah: "Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk..."
- Maintaining cleanliness and wudu
- Staying humble and focused

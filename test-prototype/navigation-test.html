How to Use

  • Set Position: Click anywhere on the canvas to move your position (red dot)
  • Navigate: Drag to pan, scroll to zoom
  • Calculate Route: Click "Select From", click a node, then "Select To", click another node, then "Calculate Route"
  • View Details: Your nearest node and distance are shown in real-time
  • Change Floor: Use the floor selector to view different floors
  • Reset: Click "Reset View" to center the graph

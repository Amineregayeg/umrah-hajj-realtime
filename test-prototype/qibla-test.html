About Qibla Direction

  • Qibla is the direction Muslims face during prayer, towards the Kaaba in Mecca
  • The direction is calculated using the great-circle distance between your location and the Kaaba
  • Direction is measured in degrees from North (0° = North, 90° = East, 180° = South, 270° = West)
  • The Kaaba is located at coordinates: 21.4225°N, 39.8262°E

Testing Instructions

  • Enter latitude and longitude manually, or use "Use My Location" for automatic detection
  • The compass will animate to show the Qibla direction from your location
  • Green arrow points toward the Kaaba, red indicates the opposite direction
